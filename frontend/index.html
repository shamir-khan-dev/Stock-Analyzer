      Trading Terms Glossary

      Clear, direct definitions for quantitative parameters and risk concepts

      Bearish Regime

      Downside Market Momentum

      Definition: Sellers hold market momentum and price is moving downward in an active selling cycle.

      Action: If holding shares, evaluate protective stops. If looking to buy, await established support stabilization.
      Bullish Regime

      Upside Market Momentum

      Definition: Buyers dominate volume flows and push price through ascending resistance levels.

      Action: Established trend favors long continuation or disciplined entry on pullbacks to support.
      Stop Loss Boundary

      Risk Invalidation Level

      Definition: A strict mathematical exit point where a thesis is proven invalid, capping capital risk.

      Rule: Never execute without a pre defined stop level. Preserving capital is the highest priority.
      Take Profit Levels

      Target Realization Thresholds

      Definition: Specific resistance or ATR expansion milestones designated for systematic profit taking.

      Strategy: Scale out 50% at Primary Target and trail stops on the remainder toward Secondary Target.
      Confluence Score

      Agreement Across Factors

      Definition: Multi factor quantitative model scoring trend, momentum, volatility, volume, and sentiment from 0 to 100.

      Scale: +50 to +100 signals strong bullish alignment; -50 to -100 signals strong bearish pressure.
      RSI Oscillator

      Relative Strength Velocity

      Definition: Momentum oscillator measuring magnitude of recent price changes on a 0 to 100 scale.

      Context: Below 30 indicates oversold conditions; above 70 indicates extended momentum conditions.
      ATR Volatility

      Average True Range

      Definition: Measures typical dollar volatility per session to calibrate realistic stop distances and profit targets.

      Usage: Ensures stops reside safely outside expected random market noise.
      Position Sizing

      Volatility Adjusted Capital Allocation

      Definition: Mathematical share calculation restricting total dollar loss to exactly 1% or 2% of total equity.

      Rule: Maintain consistent risk budgeting across every trade regardless of stock price.
