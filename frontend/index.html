      Trading Terms in Plain English

      Simple, zero-jargon definitions so you always understand what's happening

      🐻 BEARISH (DOWNTREND)

      Price Expected to Fall ⬇️

      What it means: Sellers are in control and the stock is dropping. Think of a bear swiping its claws downward.

      What to do: If you own shares, consider selling or cutting losses. If looking to buy, wait until the price stops dropping.
      🐂 BULLISH (UPTREND)

      Price Expected to Rise ⬆️

      What it means: Buyers are eager and pushing the price higher. Think of a bull charging with horns upward.

      What to do: Good time to buy or hold your shares to ride the upward wave.
      🛑 STOP-LOSS

      Your Emergency Seatbelt

      What it means: A safety price cutoff. If the stock falls to this price, you sell immediately to prevent a catastrophic loss.

      Rule: Never trade without one. A 5% loss is easy to recover; a 50% loss takes a 100% gain just to break even!
      🎯 TAKE PROFIT (TP1 / TP2)

      Your Money Target

      What it means: The price where you sell to lock in real cash profits before the stock drops back down.

      Strategy: Sell 50% of your position at TP1 to bank guaranteed profit, and let the rest run toward TP2.
      🧠 CONFLUENCE SCORE

      Agreement Between Indicators

      What it means: Instead of guessing from one chart line, our system checks 5 separate factors. If 4 or 5 agree, confidence is high.

      Scale: +50 to +100 is Bullish. -50 to -100 is Bearish. Near 0 is Neutral (sideways chop).
      ⚡ RSI (RELATIVE STRENGTH)

      The Price Speedometer (0 to 100)

      What it means: Measures how fast people are buying or selling.

      Cheat Sheet: Below 30 = Oversold (discount bargain). Above 70 = Overbought (expensive / overheated). 40–60 = Normal.
      🌊 ATR (VOLATILITY)

      Average Daily Price Swing

      What it means: Average True Range shows how many dollars the stock bounces around on an average day.

      Usage: Helps calculate how far away your Stop-Loss needs to be so normal daily wiggles don't trigger it.
      💼 POSITION SIZING

      How Many Shares to Buy

      What it means: Calculates the exact share count so you never risk more than 2% of your account on any single trade.

      Golden Rule: Protect your account so one bad trade never blows up your savings.
